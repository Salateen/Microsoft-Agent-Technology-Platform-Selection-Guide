      Alternative Platforms to Consider

      Copilot Chat Agent Builder

      Quick & Personal

      Difficulty: Very Low (No coding)
      Persona: End users, Business analysts
      Best for: Rapid personal or small-team copilots, FAQs, lightweight knowledge bots
      Knowledge: Up to 4 websites, 20 SharePoint sites
      Custom LLM: No
      Sharing: Link sharing with M365 users
      Typical time to value: Near-instant (Minutes to Hours)

      Copilot Studio

      Balanced & Scalable

      Difficulty: Low-Medium (Drag & drop)
      Persona: Business users, Makers, Low-code Developers
      Best for: Org-ready copilots (employee/customer-facing) with enterprise connectors, governance, department and LoB solutions, Conversational agents with dialog flows ,Multi-channel customer service
      Knowledge: 1000+ connectors, SharePoint, websites
      Custom LLM: Via Azure AI Foundry)
      Sharing: Teams, web, mobile apps
      Typical time to value: Moderate (Hours to Weeks)

      Azure AI Foundry

      Enterprise & Advanced

      Difficulty: High (Coding required)
      Persona: Developers, Data scientists
      Best for: Engineering bespoke AI apps/agents with full model lifecycle control, maximum flexibility in infrastructure and deployment controls
      Knowledge: Any data source
      Custom LLM: Yes, (11000+ models)
      Sharing: Enterprise endpoints, containers
      Typical time to value: Extended (Weeks to Months)
      Cost Overview: Chat Builder: Included with M365 | Studio: Starts from $200/month for 25,000 messages/month | Foundry: Consumption-based
      Platforms can be combined for comprehensive AI strategy
